Alicia Quiñonero Villora joined IVI RMA – IVI Foundation in 2005 as a Research Laboratory Technician. She has extensive expertise in molecular biology techniques applied to basic and translational research in the field of reproductive medicine.

Throughout her professional career, she has contributed to numerous international scientific publications in the field of human reproduction and has participated in a range of competitive R&D&I research projects funded by public and private institutions. Her work has focused on the application of molecular biology approaches to advance the understanding of biological mechanisms underlying human reproduction.
