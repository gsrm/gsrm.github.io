Dr. Diana Martí is a biotechnologist specialized in Assisted Human Reproduction. She conducted her doctoral research in the Department of Genomic and Systems Reproductive Medicine at IVI Foundation–La Fe Health Research Institute from 2020 to 2025, supported by a University Teacher Training (FPU) predoctoral fellowship funded by the Spanish Ministry of Science, Innovation and Universities.  

Her doctoral research focused on investigating the effects of stress on the endometrial hormonal microenvironment and gene expression, as well as the potential impact of these changes on IVF outcomes. During her doctoral training, she also contributed to several research projects that provided her with multidisciplinary research experience and fostered her professional development. This work resulted in contributions to scientific publications and presentations at scientific conferences.  

Since 2025, she has continued her research in the same department as a postdoctoral researcher.
