Inmaculada Pérez Prieto PhD is currently conducting her research activity at IIS La Fe/IVI Foundation, in the group led by Dr. Diaz-Gimeno, under a Juan de la Cierva postdoctoral fellowship. Her research focuses on the endometrial microbiome in the context of reproductive medicine. She completed her PhD in Biomedicine in 2023 under an FPU predoctoral fellowship at the University of Granada. Her doctoral thesis focused on investigating the role of the microbiome in women’s reproductive health and its association with physical activity and exercise.

During her PhD, she undertook international research stays at the Statens Serum Institut (Denmark) and the University of California, Irvine (USA), resulting in two scientific publications. One of these studies received the Basic Science Award at the 39th Annual Meeting of the European Society of Human Reproduction and Embryology (ESHRE) for her study on the gut microbiome and endometriosis (Pérez-Prieto et al., 2024, BMC Medicine). From 2024 to 2025, she worked as a postdoctoral researcher at the Hvidovre Hospital (Copenhagen, Denmark), supported by the Ramón Areces Foundation’s Postdoctoral Fellowship in Life and Matter Sciences. During this period, she focused on investigating microbiome dynamics across different body sites throughout the menstrual cycle in reproductive-aged women, involving metagenomics, metagenome-assembled genome analysis and the study of the estrobolome.

She has been PI of the project PPJIB2022-62 at the University of Granada and has participated in six national research projects focused on the microbiome. In addition to her research activity, she coordinated a teaching innovation project (FIDO Plan No. 22-229), a service-learning project developed at the University of Granada and Virgen de las Nieves University Hospital. Her teaching experience includes 17.62 ECTS credits in Bachelor degrees, and she has co-supervised one Bachelor and two Master’s theses
