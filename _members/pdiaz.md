Patricia Diaz-Gimeno is a biologist specialised in genetics, winner of the extraordinary award on the degree programme (2006). She obtained her PhD degree, working in IVI Foundation, after developing a genomic diagnostic tool based on  the transcriptomic signature for human endometrial receptivity (Diaz-Gimeno, 2011). This translational research tool was patented and nowadays is used in clinical practice. After 5 years of learning about the endometrial transcriptomics of patient population, the tool was retrained (Diaz-Gimeno, 2017) and have been became a resource of knowledge. 

Patricia Diaz-Gimeno has been specialized in highthoughtput technology and its related computational analysis. She developed a postdoctoral stage in the computational genomics department leaded by Joaquin Dopazo (2011-2013), a three months postdoctoral stay in Genomics of gene expression lab leaded by Ana Conesa (2014), both in Centro de Investigaciones Principe Felipe, and recently a six months postdoctoral stay in Oxford University UK. Nuffield department of Women's health leaded by Dagan Wells (2019). Patricia Diaz-Gimeno currently develops her research at Hospital Universitario y Politécnico La FE (HUP La Fe) and Instituto de Investigación Sanitaria (IIS) La Fe as an established principal investigator. 

Diaz-Gimeno PhD contributes to the clinical research projects at reproductive medicine unit of HUP La Fe. Diaz-Gimeno PhD has focused her research in personalised reproductive medicine based on Genomic and Systems Biology approaches using artificial intelligence (AI) and biological networks for developing new strategies for patient stratification and the proposal of new treatments respectively. She is specialised in Functional genomics, Human Reproductive Biology and in-silico biomedicine approaches. She is a co-author in around 63 original peer review publications in journals in the first quantile in reproductive medicine. Also, she has concluded 7 thesis as co-director and currently co-directs 5 PhD thesis more.

Patricia Diaz-Gimeno has published a total of 63 scientific articles having 4505 citations and more than 70 communications in the main human reproduction international conferences, that results in a total of 27 h index and a 39 i10 index (Scholar), a citation percentile 81st (WebOfScience, WOS), and a FWCI of 2.16, implying more than double citations than expected for the same area and period. Most of these articles have been published in journals with the highest impact in human reproduction research (more than 90% of articles are published in the top 25% journals by CiteScore). The article describing the endometrial evaluation signature based on artificial intelligence and transcriptomics has a total of 827 citations and the methodology is currently utilised in the clinical practice under patent EP2333107 (A4).
