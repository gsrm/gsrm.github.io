Francisco José Sanz López (PhD) is currently working in the Genomics and Systems Reproductive Medicine department, in La Fe Health Research Institute and IVI Foundation, led by Dr. Patricia Díaz Gimeno. This is a multidisciplinary group specialized in massive data analysis through the combined application of genomics and clinical approaches. Francisco José is part of the ENDOAGE project, which is aimed to study if endometrial molecular age can be used as a prognostic biomarker of reproductive success in assisted reproduction treatments. In addition, he also collaborates in the in vitro validation of results obtained in different projects in which the group is working. The author has been funded by Instituto de Salud Carlos III (Ministry of Science and Innovation) through Sara Borrell contract (CD23/00032), and also has been granted as IP by Generalitat Valenciana (CIGE-2023/117; Subvenciones para grupos de investigación emergentes).  

The author developed his PhD thesis entitled “Search of compounds with therapeutic potential for Parkinson’s disease and study of the link between Parkinson’s disease and Diabetes Mellitus” in the Molecular Genetics of Development and Biomedical Models (Department of Genetics in the University of Valencia), supervised by Dr. Nuria Paricio Ortiz. In this thesis, a high-throughput screening assay was carried out in order to identify new and more effective candidate compounds to treat Parkinson’s disease (PD) using a Drosophila model. Candidate compounds were further validated in a human cell PD model (Sanz et al. 2021). This work was carried out in collaboration with the Polymers Therapeutic Laboratory (Centro de Investigación Principe Felipe), led by Dr. Maria Jesús Vicent. In addition, the thesis also included a study aimed to evaluate the likely link between PD and type 2 Diabetes Mellitus using Drosophila models of both diseases (Sanz et al. 2022). This work was carried out in collaboration with the University Institute of Biomedical and Healthcare Research (University of Las Palmas de Gran Canaria), led by Dr. Silvia Muñoz. Besides, the author have participated in the work performed by other members of the group in which new biomarkers and pathological pathways for PD were identified (Solana-Manrique et al. 2020, 2021, 2022).  

Dr. Francisco José Sanz López has published a total of 16 scientific papers, 3 reviews and 2 book chapters, having 468 citations, resulting in a total of 11 h index and 11 i10 index. Most of articles are published in journals in the Q1 according jcr index, several of them in the d1.
