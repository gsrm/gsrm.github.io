Jose Benedito is a biologist with a specialization in biomedical sciences and a master’s degree in bioinformatics and biostatistics. He has just completed the master’s program in Assisted Human Reproductive Biotechnology offered by the University of Valencia in collaboration with IVI-RMA.  

He joined the IVI Foundation in 2025 to complete an extracurricular internship and a master’s thesis focused on the influence of variations in body mass index on age-related adverse effects on the endometrium.  

He currently works as a research assistant at IVI-RMA on a project to identify preventive and non-invasive markers of infertility associated with a genetic predisposition to anxiety and stress, and to correlate these markers with reproductive outcomes.
