Patricia Sebastián León PhD develop her current research activity in  in Genomics and Systems Reproductive Medicine department, IVI-RMA Research Foundation, led by Patricia Díaz Gimeno, PhD in La Fe Health Research Institute since 2016, funded from 2022 to 2024 by ISCII (Sara Borrell grant).  In this period, she has contributed, as data-scientist, in projects related with the analysis of massive endometrial transcriptomic data in the context of reproductive medicine. In particular, her research is focused in the study of the posible causes of implantation failure from different omics perpectives (transcriptomics, metabolomics and epigenetics). In addition, she had co-supervised one master's thesis in Genetics and Genomics and two master's thesis in Bioinformatics.  

The author developed her PhD thesis in Functional Genomics department, Centro de Investigación Principe Felipe (CIPF), led by Joaquin Dopazo Blázquez PhD. Her thesis describe a new methodology for the statistical modeling and analysis of signaling pathways based on massive transcriptomics data (Sebastian-Leon et al. 2013, 2014). These signaling pathway models developed by the author were used in other two works, related to the analysis of massive variant calling data (Hernansaiz-Ballesteros et al. 2015) and the use of massive transcriptomic data for signaling pathway predictive models based on machine learning algorithms (Amadoz, et al. 2015). The developed methodologies were tested in the context of two international challenges: CAMDA, were comparison of in-vivo and in-vitro drug response methodology was granted for a long-talk in the CAMDA congress; and DREAM, were the prediction of toxicity of different compounds were selected to be included in the paper (Eduati et al. 2015).

Her PhD and a short-time stay in Bioinformatics School (Edinburgh University) were founded by Spanish Government FPI grant (BES-2009-015788). During her PhD, she co-supervised a master's thesis in Bioinformatics. Over the next two years, the author took an active part in STATegra European project by developing a Bioconductor’s package for the analysis and integration of different sources of massive data (Planell et al. 2020) and participating as lecturer in four courses of analysis and integration of massive data. In addition, two short-stays were done in the context of this project: one in  Statistics Department of Amsterdam University (COST european grant) (van der Kloet et al. 2016) and other in Instituto Nacional de Medicina Genomica (INMEGEN), in Mexico City (DEANN international grant).
