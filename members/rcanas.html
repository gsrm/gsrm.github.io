Dr. Rubén Cañas-Cañas is a researcher specializing in Bioinformatics and Data Science, holding a PhD in Medicine and Translational Research (Cum Laude). He currently supports large-scale genomic research at the IVI Institute through the design and management of high-performance computing (HPC) infrastructures and scalable NGS workflows.

His core research interests lie at the intersection of genomics, metagenomics, and data analysis. Rubén has actively contributed to the scientific community with publications in the field of wastewater-based epidemiology, focusing on SARS-CoV-2 dynamics and antibiotic resistance surveillance, as well as research on the genetic basis of neurodegenerative diseases.
